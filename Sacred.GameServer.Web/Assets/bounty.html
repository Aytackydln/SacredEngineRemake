Bounties

The board draws from this list, never the same entry twice at once. Creatures match whole words of a creature's name (needs --game-dir); pick a group or a creature, or add another word; the target is drawn between From and To. Item quality: 0 is plain, a higher number is rarer; "No item" pays gold only. The category narrows the item (any item by default). Hours empty: the default lifetime.

Stored in the mod's storage (key config); a change applies within 2 seconds, no restart or reload. Bounties already on the board keep their values; the script file's values only seed a fresh install.
